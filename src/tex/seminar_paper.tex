\author{Sebastian Frehmel}

\englishabstract{3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) denotes a seminal approach to 3D reconstruction and novel-view synthesis which was first published in 2023. This paper introduces the underlying motivation, algorithms and concepts of 3DGS in full detail as of the original publication. We conclude by presenting a selection of applications of 3DGS, focussing on dynamic scene reconstruction as a more in-depth example.}
