\englishabstract{3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) denotes a seminal approach to 3D reconstruction and novel-view synthesis which was first published in 2023. We introduce the underlying motivation, mechanisms and concepts of 3DGS as of the original publication. We then proceed to show algorithmic advancements in the three years since and focus on 3DGS Video to illustrate a practical application thereof.}
